When your tuning file service grows beyond a local region, you face a new set of challenges: time zones, multiple languages, currency conversions, and network hierarchies.

## The Challenge

"Global Tuning Network" (GTN) had reached a plateau. With 150 active slaves across Europe, their 4 Master Tuners were overwhelmed. 
More importantly, GTN wanted to expand into South America and Asia, but they couldn't hire local support staff or manage the billing complexity manually.

They needed a way to empower local "Resellers" to manage their own regions, while GTN handled the core file processing in the background.

## The Solution

GTN upgraded to the **TunerPortal Enterprise Tier** and launched their own fully White Labeled portal.

1. **Custom Branding:** They mapped `portal.globaltuningnetwork.com` to TunerPortal. Their logo, brand colors, and custom emails were fully integrated.

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/withelabel_light.png" dark="/screenshots/withelabel_dark.png" alt="White Label Portal" />

2. **Reseller Management:** They identified their top 5 customers globally and upgraded their accounts to "Reseller" status.
3. **Credit Transfers:** These Resellers bought credits from GTN in bulk (e.g., 5,000 credits for €15,000). The Resellers then used the TunerPortal dashboard to transfer smaller amounts of credits to their local sub-dealers at a markup.

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/Reseller_light.png" dark="/screenshots/Reseller_dark.png" alt="Reseller Management" />

4. **AI Support Routing:** To handle the different time zones, GTN activated the TunerPortal AI Support Agent. It was trained on their specific pricing and tool compatibility logic, automatically answering 60% of inbound tickets while the European Master Tuners were asleep.

## The Results

By shifting the administrative burden to their Resellers via TunerPortal's tiered hierarchy, GTN scaled from 150 slaves to **over 1,200 active slaves globally** in 18 months.

Their file volume increased by 800%, while their administrative overhead actually decreased. The White Label platform allowed them to look like a multi-million euro software company, building massive trust in new markets.

**"The White Label portal was the catalyst for our global expansion. It handles the complexity so we can focus on the calibrations."** - *CEO, Global Tuning Network*
