Running a high-end tuning shop with a four-wheel dyno means you are constantly booked. When you have cars physically waiting on the dyno, every minute counts.

## The Challenge

"Performance Auto Works" was processing 15-20 cars a day in their physical shop, while also supporting a small network of 5 B2B slaves remotely.
Their two Master Tuners were spending up to 3 hours a day just downloading files from WhatsApp, checking if invoices were paid, and manually naming and organizing files in Windows Explorer. 

When a slave sent a file with missing hardware numbers, the Master Tuner had to stop their dyno session, text the slave, and wait for a reply.

## The Solution

They implemented TunerPortal as their central nervous system.

1. **Mandatory Portal Usage:** They stopped accepting files via WhatsApp. All slaves and local shop technicians were required to upload original files through the TunerPortal dashboard.
2. **Auto-Detection:** TunerPortal automatically extracted the ECU data from the binary files, instantly displaying the HW/SW numbers to the Master Tuners.

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/admin_platform_light.png" dark="/screenshots/admin_platform_Dark.png" alt="Admin Business Dashboard" />

3. **Automated Queue:** Files appeared in a live dashboard on the wall of the tuning room. The system automatically prioritized files based on SLA (e.g., Dyno Priority vs. Standard B2B).
4. **Pre-Paid Credits:** B2B slaves had to buy credits upfront via Stripe. No more chasing unpaid €100 invoices at the end of the month.

## The Results

By eliminating the administrative chaos, the two Master Tuners regained **3 hours per day**.
They used this reclaimed time to develop custom Stage 3 calibrations and increase their daily throughput. The business scaled from 20 cars a day to 35 cars a day without hiring additional tuning staff.

**"TunerPortal didn't just organize our files; it gave us our time back."** - *Lead Master Tuner, Performance Auto Works*
