Your tuning expertise brings customers to your door, but the software experience is what keeps them coming back. If your B2B customers have to chase you down on WhatsApp for file updates, they will eventually switch to a provider with a smoother workflow.

A dedicated **Customer Portal** transforms your file service from a chaotic inbox into a professional SaaS platform.

## The Dashboard Experience

When your B2B customers (slaves) log in, they don't see a generic upload form. They see a premium, enterprise-grade dashboard.

### Key Features of the Customer Portal

* **Wallet & Credits:** Customers instantly see their credit balance. When they run low, they can purchase more credits with a single click using their preferred payment method.
* **Upload Wizard:** A seamless flow for uploading original files, inputting vehicle details, and selecting requested services (Stage 1, DPF OFF, Vmax, etc.).

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/fileupload_light.png" dark="/screenshots/fileupload_dark.png" alt="File Upload Wizard" />

* **Live File Tracking:** Customers can track the exact status of their file (Pending, In Progress, Ready, Needs Info).
* **Integrated Support Chat:** If a customer has a question about a specific file, they can open a chat directly attached to that file ID. No more confusion over which car they are talking about.
* **Invoice History:** All invoices are automatically generated and stored in the portal for easy accounting.
