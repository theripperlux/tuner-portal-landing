## The Problem with Email and WhatsApp

Many master tuners start by accepting files via WhatsApp or email. This works for 5 customers, but fails at 50.
* Files get lost or mismatched.
* Invoices are sent late or forgotten entirely.
* Support queries are mixed in with personal messages.
* There is no central database of previous tunes.

## The TunerPortal Solution

TunerPortal was engineered from the ground up to solve the exact bottlenecks file providers face every day.

### 1. Centralized File Queue
All incoming original files are routed into a central, live-updating queue. Your master tuners can simply click "Claim", download the file, tune it, and upload it back. The customer is notified instantly.

### 2. Built-in Credit Wallet & Invoicing
Stop acting like a bank. With TunerPortal, customers must purchase credits *before* they can request a tune. Once the tune is delivered, the credits are deducted automatically, and a legally compliant PDF invoice is generated.

### 3. WinOLS & Tool Integration
Our platform speaks the language of the tuning industry. We integrate directly with the APIs of WinOLS, AutoTuner, Alientech (Kess3), and Magicmotorsport (Flex). This allows for seamless file transfer without manual downloading and uploading.
