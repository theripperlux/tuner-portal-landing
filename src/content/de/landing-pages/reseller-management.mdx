When a tuning file service scales globally, you can no longer manage every individual slave yourself. The most successful networks rely on a **Reseller Model**. 

You sell high volumes of tuning credits to a Master Tuner in another country, and they act as the local distributor for your files. Managing this complex hierarchy requires specialized software.

## The Reseller Hierarchy

TunerPortal supports deep organizational structures to model real-world tuning networks.

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/Reseller_light.png" dark="/screenshots/Reseller_dark.png" alt="Reseller Hierarchy and Network" />

1. **The Administrator (You):** You own the portal, set the global prices, and manage the infrastructure.
2. **The Reseller:** A high-volume partner who buys credits in bulk at a steep discount (e.g., Tier 1 Pricing).
3. **The Sub-Dealer:** A mechanic or slave who buys credits from the Reseller at a markup.

## Reseller Features

### Bulk Credit Transfers
Resellers can easily transfer credits from their main wallet into the wallets of their sub-dealers. This allows the Reseller to manage their own billing locally while relying on your portal for the actual file processing.

### Tiered Pricing Structures

<Screenshot light="/screenshots/pricing_light.png" dark="/screenshots/pricing_dark.png" alt="Pricing Management" />

Set up dynamic pricing tiers. A standard customer might pay 10 credits for a Stage 1 tune, while a Gold Tier Reseller only pays 6 credits. As their volume increases, the system automatically upgrades their tier, incentivizing loyalty.

### Multi-Language Support
Global networks require global languages. TunerPortal supports 12 languages out of the box, ensuring that your Reseller in Spain and their sub-dealer in Italy can both use the platform comfortably.

## Scale Globally

Managing a reseller network manually is impossible. Credits get lost, pricing errors occur, and relationships suffer.

By utilizing TunerPortal's advanced Reseller Management features, you can securely and automatically scale your tuning business to a global level.
