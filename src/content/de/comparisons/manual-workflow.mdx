Many master tuners start their business using manual tools: WhatsApp for communication, Email for file transfer, and Excel for invoicing. 

While this works for your first 5 customers, it completely breaks down when you scale. Here is how a manual workflow compares to using **TunerPortal**.

## 1. File Reception & Extraction

**Manual Workflow:** 
A slave messages you on WhatsApp: "Can you do Stage 1 on a Golf 7 GTI?" You have to tell them to send the file via Email. You download the attachment, manually create a folder, open the file in WinOLS to identify the hardware and software numbers, and write them down.
*Time wasted: 15 minutes per file.*

**TunerPortal:** 
The slave uploads the file to their branded portal. TunerPortal automatically extracts the ECU Type, Hardware Number, and Software Number in milliseconds. The file appears instantly in your dashboard queue.
*Time wasted: 0 minutes.*

## 2. Invoicing and Payments

**Manual Workflow:** 
After you finish tuning the file, you ask the slave to send €100 via PayPal. They promise to do it tomorrow. You send the file anyway because you trust them. Two weeks later, you realize they never paid. You have to manually create an invoice in your accounting software.
*Revenue lost: High.*

**TunerPortal:** 
The slave must have sufficient Credits in their digital wallet *before* they can even upload the file. When you deliver the file, the credits are automatically deducted. TunerPortal generates a legal PDF invoice instantly and emails it to the customer.
*Revenue lost: €0.*

## 3. Customer Support

**Manual Workflow:** 
You are working on a complex Stage 3 tune, and your phone buzzes 10 times. Five different slaves are asking if their file is ready. You have to stop tuning, check the status of each file, and reply manually.

**TunerPortal:** 
Slaves can see the exact, live status of their file in the portal ("Pending", "In Progress", "Ready"). If they have a technical question, the **TunerPortal AI Agent** analyzes the file and answers them instantly. Your phone stays silent.

## The Verdict

A manual workflow limits your tuning business to a maximum of 10-15 files a day before you burn out. 

With TunerPortal, the system handles the admin work, allowing you to easily scale to 50+ files a day without hiring extra staff.
